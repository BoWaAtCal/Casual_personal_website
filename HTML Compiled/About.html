❓ Me Beyond the Resume

I was born in 2006 to Chinese parents in Warabi, the smallest city by land area in Japan 🇯🇵, allowing me to engage with aspects of both Chinese and Japanese cultures on a deeply personal level within the tight-knit community. (did take forever to get to Tokyo though)

I've been playing soccer at the varsity/intramural level for the past 8 years, primarily as a goalkeeper 🧤 and striker. I also greatly enjoy listening to C/J/K-pop music as well as learning about novel domains including ancient history 🏛️, cognition, and such.

Personally, I've been invested in numerous domains outside of the scope of traditional software and data, including an economics research project that attempts to predict Asian GDP growth 📊, a podcast that records stories and experiences in rural Japan, and the "Bright Star" initiative in China aimed at integrating those with autism 🧸 into the local community.
